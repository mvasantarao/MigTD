## Decision and provenance

The user directed replacement of bilateral migration contributions,
commitments and HKDF with **one source-generated fresh random 32-byte MSK**,
and replacement of planned-handoff seed/HKDF with an **independent
outgoing-generated fresh random 32-byte secret**. The user separately selected
**SPDM-protected delivery acceptance receipts, without application HMAC
confirmations**. These are 256-bit secrets, not 32-bit values.

- **AMD:** ARG v1.20 section 8.2 accepts a supplied MSK32, algorithm `0x0003`
  and migration data64; sender firmware role is 0, receiver 1. Sections
  3.3.2/8.3 support outgoing-generated, securely transferred handoff secrets.
  Section 3.3.3 incoming-only recovery remains unchanged.
- **TDX precedent:** the checked rebind token uses `SystemRandom` random32,
  and migration-key owners use zeroization. TDX migration itself reads module
  migration keys; it is not evidence that TDX uses this SNP single-MSK protocol.
- **DESIGN:** single-generator selection, the V2 application records, purpose
  identifiers, hashes and scheduling below are application choices, not
  AMD-specified wire messages or kernel/GHCB interfaces.

The source is trusted for MSK entropy/key selection. Bilateral entropy input
and precommitment are relinquished. The destination accepts the protected
key/context; this is **not cryptographic proof of application key possession**
and **not proof of firmware installation or attested persistence**.
Both authorized MAs necessarily possess the MSK. No HKDF can prevent a
compromised participating MA from disclosing it.

## Preserved invariants

Mutual current-TH1-bound evidence and fixed/ordered policy appraisal, complete
tenant evidence, source `REPORT_ID_MA` association, both-role authorization,
independently admitted byte-identical history64, anchored replay/state
admission, role-correct firmware operands, deadlines, zeroization, cleanup and
partial/unknown-effect reconciliation remain required.

Do not regenerate a replacement secret on timeout, retry, restart or uncertain
firmware effect. Retain the same operation-owned secret for an explicitly
authorized retry; otherwise fail/reconcile. Constructor shape checks do not
provide durable once-only generation or restart authority. Those obligations
remain in runtime/state-provider integration. No empty-journal restart,
plaintext fallback, replacement live SPDM session or fake successful verifier.

## Version separation and evidence

The 220-byte `ReportDataContextV1` container and full64 REPORT_DATA construction
remain unchanged. Its purpose registry gains:

| Purpose | Meaning | Roles / algorithm |
|---|---|---|
| `0x06` | V2 inter-node session evidence | Source, destination / `0x0003` |
| `0x07` | V2 reciprocal tenant authorization | Source, destination / `0x0003` |

Purposes 1/2 and contribution/confirmation records retain their historical
meanings; they cannot be reinterpreted as V2. Planned/recovery purposes 4/5
remain unchanged, and fresh persisted-state purpose3 remains deferred.

For purpose6:

```text
binding = SHA384(
  "SNP-LM-SESSION-BIND-v2" ||
  request_id:u64le || negotiated_migration_version:u16le || algorithm:u16le ||
  tenant_REPORT_ID[32] || complete_tenant_evidence_digest[48] || actual_TH1[48])
REPORT_DATA = SHA384(exact_context[220]) || zero[16]
```

Role and local MA/policy identity are bound by the full context, not an
attested secret commitment. The expected context is reconstructed locally.
Legacy purpose1 evidence or arbitrary binding digests cannot mint V2 proofs.

The exact V2 authorization preimage is **325 bytes**:

```text
"SNP-LM-TENANT-AUTH-v2" || version:u16le=2 ||
request_id:u64le || migration_version:u16le || algorithm:u16le=3 ||
source_role:u8=0 || destination_role:u8=1 ||
tenant_REPORT_ID[32] || complete_tenant_evidence_digest[48] || TH1[48] ||
source_MA_REPORT_ID[32] || destination_MA_REPORT_ID[32] ||
source_MA_POLICY_IDENTITY_DIGEST[48] || destination_MA_POLICY_IDENTITY_DIGEST[48]
```

No commitment fields or zero-filled commitment placeholders. Purpose7 binds
`SHA384(authorization_preimage)` and the existing per-role context. Both fresh
role proofs must match the same authorization and previously verified MA
REPORT_IDs. The V2 digest feeds the existing durable replay capability; old
bindings conflict rather than silently upgrading persisted state.

The independently reconstructed **703-byte nonsecret session preimage** is:

```text
"SNP-LM-SESSION-v2" || version:u16le=2 ||
authorization_preimage[325] ||
source_complete_MA_evidence_digest[48] || destination_complete_MA_evidence_digest[48] ||
tenant_collateral_digest[48] || exact_admitted_history[64] ||
trust:u16le || interpretation:u16le || tcb_profile:u8=3 || approval_profile:u16le ||
source_approval_digest[48] || destination_approval_digest[48] ||
initial_authorization_digest[48]
```

Trust 1 is the isolated pinned test root, 2 Azure production. Interpretation
1/2 denotes raw/supplemented complete evidence. Approval profile 1/2 denotes
fixed/ordered releases; initial authorization is zero only for fixed profile,
and required nonzero for ordered. Complete evidence/approval hashes must come
from the admitted local claims adapter, including every authenticated
supplement byte. Computing this recipe is not itself policy/history admission.

Session reference = request ID plus SHA384(preimage), never a SPDM session ID,
TH1 alone, authorization digest alone or a secret/key fingerprint.

## Active protected wire and ordering

Existing evidence/collateral/tenant/result opcodes `0x20`-`0x25` retain their
record containers, with V2 evidence purposes enforced by the peer boundary.
The old key/confirmation/completion opcodes `0x26`-`0x2D` are reserved
historical allocations and rejected by the active shared layout lookup.
They are not a downgrade/fallback. `0x2E`/`0x2F` remain unallocated.

| Opcode | Direction and meaning | Element | Standalone type | Bytes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0x30` | Source: final key delivery | `0x0109` | `0x000A` | 168 |
| `0x31` | Destination: accepted delivery/context | `0x010A` | `0x000B` | 80 |
| `0x32` | Source: poll destination firmware completion | `0x010C` | `0x000D` | 80 |
| `0x33` | Destination: correlated firmware outcome | `0x010B` | `0x000C` | 88 |
| `0x34` | Source: correlated firmware outcome | `0x010B` | `0x000C` | 88 |
| `0x35` | Destination: acknowledge source completion | `0x010C` | `0x000D` | 80 |

VDM framing remains major/minor0/0, one element and total <=8192 bytes.
All scalar fields below are little-endian. Every new standalone header is:

```text
magic[8] || version:u16=2 || type:u16 || exact_length:u32
session_reference = request_id:u64 || session_context_digest[48]
```

- Key (`SNP-LM-K`, type10): header16 + reference56 + **final MSK32** +
  exact admitted history64. Decoder wipes its caller buffer on success/failure;
  owners/frame buffers zeroize and redact diagnostics.
- Acceptance (`SNP-LM-A`, type11): header16 + reference56 +
  destination role:u8=1 + accepted:u8=1 + reserved zero6.
- Firmware outcome (`SNP-LM-F`, type12): header16 + reference56 + role:u8 +
  outcome:u8 + effect:u8 + status-present:u8 + raw-status:u32 + reserved zero8.
  Outcome 1/2/3/4 = completed/not-applied/unavailable/indeterminate;
  effect 0/1/2/3 = not-applied/applied/partial/unknown. Completed requires
  applied and present status0; unavailable requires not-applied/absent status;
  not-applied cannot carry status0; indeterminate requires partial/unknown.
- Control (`SNP-LM-Q`, type13): header16 + reference56 + kind:u8 +
  reserved zero7. Kind1 polls destination completion; kind2 acknowledges source
  completion. Absent raw status is encoded as presence0/value0, not success.

The promoted, current-binding/policy-checked peer channel alone can mint
`ProtectedInstallRecordV2`, after successful I/O. Decoded records cannot.
`InstallExchangeSequenceV2` observes all six records in order, rejects crossed
role/session/TH1/history, duplicates and gaps, and poisons on error.

After delivery/acceptance, only the destination's local receipt permits
destination installation. The source must additionally observe successful
destination firmware completion. Host installation entry checks local role
and the exact authorization digest against its durable operation binding.
Success requires successful local installation and the full correlated
exchange, including source completion/acknowledgement. Failed, partial or
unknown outcomes never authorize success. Raw firmware outcomes are not
attested persistence.
